Paper-strip music box

Thirty notes, one strip of card

A steel comb of thirty tuned teeth, and a strip of card that feeds past it. Each hole lifts one tooth and lets it ring. Click the strip to punch a hole or patch one, then turn the crank.

    The comb

    Thirty teeth, from C3 at the long bass end to E6. The comb leaves out notes tunes rarely ask for, like E3 and F3, and adds sharps in the middle octaves where melodies live. Lead weights under the lowest teeth slow them down to pitch. Click a note to hear its tooth.
