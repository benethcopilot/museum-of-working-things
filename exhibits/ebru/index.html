Turkish paper marbling

Ebru

Inks thinned with ox gall float on water thickened with gum tragacanth. The marbler sprinkles them with a horsehair brush, draws them into patterns with an awl and a comb, then lifts them off with one sheet of paper. Each tray gives one print.
