Kintsugi金継ぎ

Mend it with gold

Kintsugi is the Japanese craft of repairing broken pottery with lacquer and powdered gold. Nothing is hidden: each seam shows where the bowl broke and how it was put back together. A bowl that breaks again keeps its old seams and gains new ones.

The repair

weeks of work, here in seconds
  1. Mugi-urushi麦漆Raw lacquer mixed with wheat flour glues the shards back together.
  2. Sabi-urushi錆漆A paste of whetstone powder and lacquer fills the gaps and chips.
  3. Bengara-urushi弁柄漆A fine line of red lacquer is painted along every seam.
  4. Maki-e蒔絵Gold powder is sprinkled onto the lacquer while it is still tacky.
  5. Migaki磨きOnce it has cured, the gold is burnished until it shines.

Between steps, each layer cures for days in a warm, humid cabinet. Urushi hardens by taking up moisture, not by drying out.
